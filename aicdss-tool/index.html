About the framework

A transparency-focused governance framework

AI can support diagnosis and treatment decisions, but only if the people using it understand what it does, what it was tested on and where it can fail.

Most international guidance on AI in healthcare is written for developers and regulators in other countries. This tool turns that guidance into clear questions that a Nigerian healthcare institution can answer for itself.

Each No or I do not know answer is treated as a governance gap, and the tool explains what the institution can do to close it.

What the tool does

Clear answers, practical next steps

Designed for hospital managers, clinicians and committee members, with no specialist AI knowledge required.

Structured assessment

Answer plain-language questions about the AI-CDSS, its vendor and how it is used in your institution.

Governance gaps

See exactly where your institution lacks the information or processes it needs.

Practical guidance

Every gap comes with guidance on what to request from the vendor or put in place internally.

Before and during use

Use it before adopting a system and repeat it while the system is in use.

Shareable report

View, print or download a report for management, committees or the vendor.

Private by design

No patient data is collected. Each user can only see their own assessments.
